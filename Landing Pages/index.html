A NOTE FROM OUR KITCHEN

Not just baked.
Made with feeling.

Wildflower Bakery started in a tiny kitchen with one oven and a lot of stubborn optimism. What began as weekend baking for friends turned into a little corner where the smell of fresh bread pulls people in off the street.

We still do things the slow way. Dough that rests overnight. Butter that's never in a hurry. Every loaf, pastry, and cake is made by hand, in small batches, by people who genuinely love doing this.
