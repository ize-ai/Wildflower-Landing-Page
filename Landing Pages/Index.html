Our Story

Wildflower Bakery started in a tiny kitchen with one oven and a lot of stubborn optimism. What began as weekend baking for friends turned into something bigger — a little corner where the smell of fresh bread pulls people in off the street. We still do things the slow way. Dough that rests overnight. Butter that's never in a hurry. Nothing rushed, nothing shipped in from somewhere else. Every loaf, pastry, and cake that leaves our kitchen is made by hand, in small batches, by people who genuinely love doing this. That's the whole secret, really — no trick to it, just care.
